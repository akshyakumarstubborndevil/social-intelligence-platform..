# AI Social Intelligence & Early-Warning Platform

An AI-powered platform that doesn't just analyze what people are saying on social media, but detects emerging issues, explains why they are growing, maps how they spread, and shows the evidence behind every alert.

## Live Demo
https://claude.ai/artifact/7qkrLr758Z43VLwPgewLuz

(Open while signed in to claude.ai. Live Claude analysis runs through the viewer's Claude account.)

## Features
- Multilingual sentiment & emotion analysis (English, Telugu, Hindi, Tenglish, Hinglish)
- Trend and topic detection
- Early-warning alerts for emerging issues
- Root-cause analysis with supporting evidence
- "What changed?" period comparison
- Potential misinformation / rumor tracker
- Potential coordinated-activity detection
- Complaint-to-action engine
- Influence and network analysis
- Explainable "Why was this flagged?" evidence panel
- Short-term trend forecast

## Ethics
Uses public or anonymised data only. Signals are labelled "potential" and require human verification. No individual profiling.
